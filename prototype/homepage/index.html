About JustMaya

JustMaya turns a document into a playable lesson. Upload a PDF and it plans a learning roadmap, generates a visual-novel episode, and checks your understanding inside the story — with a tutor you can raise your hand for at any point.

Mita is the in-app guide: she narrates, teaches, and keeps you company between sessions.

This page is a design prototype — buttons run mock flows, nothing leaves your machine. The real build is React + Vite in front of a FastAPI pipeline.
